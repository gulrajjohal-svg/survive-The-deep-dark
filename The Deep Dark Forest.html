GUIDE BOOK

About: Ashen Woods is a huge open-world survival game. Giant trees, vine-choked ruins and the undead fill the forest, and the nights get worse. Explore, loot, craft, tame dogs and survive.

Goal: Survive 24 nights. On Night 25 the seal on the Ancient Ruin breaks and The Warden awakens. Kill him before dawn or you lose and must restart. Beating him unlocks Easy, Normal, Hard, Insane and Nightmare modes.

Controls: WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump, mouse look, click attack, F or right-click throw rock, E interact (chests, berries, bones, dogs), 1 eat, 2 drink, 3 medkit, 4 blue berries, 5 purple berries, B coat rock in venom, C craft medkit (2 cloth + 1 scrap), V craft ammo (3 scrap + 1 wood), G fortress, Esc guide book.

Survival: Hunger and thirst drain over time; at zero you take damage. Blue berries are safe food. Dark purple berries are venomous: eating them poisons you, but coating your rock gives 8 poisoned hits. Golden beacons mark chests holding ammo, food, water, medkits, wood, scrap, cloth and sometimes a pistol.

Weapons: a sharpened rock (melee), a boomerang rock throw that hits on the way out and back, and the pistol once you find it (needs ammo).

Undead: Zombies (red eyes, groan); Chainsaw zombies (cut down trees to reach you, drop scrap); Mutants (scream to enrage everything nearby, leap at you, go berserk when hurt); The Warden (boss: ground slams and raises the dead).

Animals (25 species):

Venomous animals poison you on a hit. Hunting animals gives food.

Dogs: Wild dogs with glowing yellow eyes want to be your friend, but they only trust you if you give them a bone. Collect bones (white sticks) inside buildings and give one to a dog with E. Tamed dogs have 90 HP, follow you and fight for you.

Ruins: Many have several floors joined by ramps, with chests on every floor. The arrows in the corner point to the nearest ruin and to the Ancient Ruin.

Fortress: Unlocks after you survive Night 15. Press G with 15 scrap to build a walled fortress with 5,000 HP and 2 guns (5 damage each). Press G near it to open or close the gate. Enemies attack its walls.

Nights and modes: A day lasts 2 minutes and nights get more dangerous. Nightmare mode adds a cave dungeon full of monsters and loot.
